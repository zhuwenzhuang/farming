## Chat state and recovery

- Synchronize completed Chat turns after background navigation and history paging without requiring a browser refresh.
- Preserve Runtime-owned permission waits and failures while requests are settling, and mark interrupted work correctly after cold recovery.
- Keep autonomous turn failures visible even when a Provider supplies no error message. Preserve unknown, failed, and cancelled subagent states, and avoid marking historical turns as running.
- Keep transcript windows continuous and bounded during long turns. Preserve historical reading positions across completion and reconnect, and obtain a fresh checkpoint when returning to the latest content.
- Reject stale responses and version regressions. Bound queued reads, timeouts, and recovery attempts, with an explicit Retry action when synchronization cannot complete.
